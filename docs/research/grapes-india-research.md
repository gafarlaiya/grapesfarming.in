# Grape Cultivation in India: Primary-Source Viticulture, Fertilizer Calculator & Export Reference (Passes 1–4)

## Executive Summary: 10 Non-Negotiable Facts for Indian Grape Growers
1. **Pick rootstock by water sodium (`Na+`), NOT blindly Dogridge:** **ICAR-NRCG Pune** proved **`Dogridge` cannot exclude Sodium**—under saline borewells it accumulates `Na+`, triggers Potassium deficiency, and causes dead cordons after Year 4–5; use **`110R` (or NRCG clone `B2-56`)** in saline/drought vineyards (`ICAR-NRCG`; `Sharma et al.`).
2. **Respect irrigation water salinity (`ECw`) & bicarbonates:** Excellent grape water has **`ECw < 1.0 dS/m`** (`>2.7 dS/m` causes severe loss) and **`Cl- <= 3 me/L`** own-rooted (`up to 18 me/L` on tolerant rootstocks); treat high-bicarbonate water (`HCO3- > 2.5–3.0 me/L`) with controlled **acidification to `pH 6.5`** so lime spots don't stain berries (`ICAR-NRCG`; `MANAGE`).
3. **April (Foundation) Pruning decides next year's bunches:** Fruit-bud differentiation happens **35–60 days after April pruning** (`Apr–May` sunlight on `0–2` basal buds); canopy shade or excess N during days `31–60` ruins October fruitfulness (`ICAR-NRCG`; `MPKV`).
4. **Time October (Forward) Pruning cleanly (`5–10` buds):** Pruning too early (`Sept`) exposes tender flower panicles to monsoon Downy Mildew; delaying into late `Nov` risks cold dormancy & shot berries (`ICAR-NRCG`; `Maharashtra Protocols 2026`).
5. **Apply Hydrogen Cyanamide (`Dormex 50% SL`) within `24–48 hrs` of October pruning:** Waiting past 48 hours slashes bud-break uniformity; follow `30–40 ml/L` (`1.5–2.0%` active) limits to avoid bud burn (`ICAR-NRCG`).
6. **Switch from Bower (`Pandal`) to `Extended Y-Trellis` or `Flat Roof Gable`:** Separating the canopy at `30°–40°` boosts cane sunlight, increases bud fruitfulness, cuts Downy/Powdery mildew microclimate humidity, and improves `GA3` spray reach (`ICAR-NRCG`; `MANAGE`).
7. **Balance crop load (`35–40` bunches/vine):** Over-cropping delays veraison/sugar (`<16°Brix`), yields small berries, and weakens next-season canes (`ICAR-NRCG`).
8. **Protect EU export eligibility via APEDA `GrapeNet` from Day 1:** EU-bound grapes require `GrapeNet` registration, **`NRL-NRCG`** multi-residue clearance, **`>=16°Brix`**, `~20:1` sugar/acid ratio, and forced-air pre-cooling (`APEDA 2026`; `NRCG`).
9. **Beware EU MRL failure triggers (`CCC`, `Phosphonates`, `Mancozeb`, `Carbendazim`):** Strictly follow **`ICAR-NRCG Annexure-5` RMP PHIs** and use mechanical sub-cane pinching (`5–7` leaves) over repeated Chlormequat Chloride sprays (`APEDA`; `ICAR-NRCG`).
10. **Verify regional land units before dosing:** **1 Acre = `40` Gunthas** (`101.17 m²` each) in Maharashtra & Gujarat (`Saurashtra vigha = 16 gunthas`, `GJ standard = 20`, `Gaekwad = 24`) (`Revenue Dept 2026`).
